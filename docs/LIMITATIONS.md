# Scope And Limitations

The paper should state these boundaries clearly.

- The evaluated perturbation is pure order reversal, used as a diagnostic of
  scan-direction bias.
- The method is not claimed to be a full reverse-complement equivariant DNA
  foundation model.
- Published Caduceus-Ph-131k and Caduceus-PS-131k are strong public baselines,
  not same-budget controls.
- The same-budget mechanism comparison is between Mamba2-1k, Mamba2-RevPh-1k,
  and MLBN-1k.
- The long-range external validation focuses on classification-style 450 kb
  tasks from DNALongBench. More complex 2D or trajectory-regression outputs are
  left to future work.
